# VITHELO B2B Homepage Design Specification

**Date:** 2026-08-24  
**Status:** Approved design, not yet implemented  
**Target:** `E:\CodexWorkspace\8-23独立站\website\VITHELO_Homepage_V2.1_footer_scroll_frames.html`

## 1. Real problem

Preserve the existing VITHELO homepage as the visual and technical baseline, then turn it into a premium B2B export website that helps U.S. nutrition buyers understand the offer, assess manufacturing fit, reduce sourcing risk, and contact sales.

The homepage is not a DTC shop, a generic factory brochure, or a product-price catalog. Its primary conversion is a direct sales conversation.

## 2. Confirmed business position

- VITHELO is the factory-owned overseas brand and export sales team.
- Primary market: United States.
- Primary buyer: overseas nutrition brands.
- Secondary buyer: cross-border e-commerce sellers.
- Third buyer: physical retailers and large supermarkets.
- Core format: gummies.
- Stable supporting formats: hard capsules, softgels, tablets, powders, liquid supplements, functional gum, and oral films.
- Primary contact routes: email and WhatsApp.
- Secondary conversion route: structured project brief.
- Site language: American English.
- No public pricing, cart, checkout, coupon, or consumer promotion logic.

## 3. Scope

### Must have

- Preserve and evolve the current homepage rather than replacing it.
- Build the homepage around a U.S. buyer decision journey.
- Make gummies the clear specialty while proving multi-format capacity.
- Explain OEM/ODM development, manufacturing, quality, MOQ, project flow, and channel fit.
- Keep email and WhatsApp continuously easy to reach.
- Use verified factory facts and visibly withhold unconfigured facts.
- Support desktop and mobile without hiding critical facts in mobile-only disclosure.

### Should have

- Dedicated pages for Gummies, dosage-form overview, OEM/ODM, Manufacturing, Quality, About, and Contact.
- Dedicated solution pages for six U.S. market directions.
- A lightweight project brief reached from the homepage.
- Real factory, product, laboratory, and packaging photography.

### Could have later

- B2B resources and SEO content center.
- Case studies with documented permission.
- Download center and inquiry guide.
- Additional languages.

### Won't have in the first version

- Online quote calculation.
- E-commerce checkout or payment.
- Customer accounts.
- Unverified customer logos or marketplace partnerships.
- AI-generated images.
- Invented claims, certifications, turnaround times, prices, or regulatory approvals.

## 4. Site architecture

The approved architecture is a buyer-decision hybrid rather than a product-only catalog or factory-only brochure.

1. **Home** — establish positioning and route buyers.
2. **Products** — Gummies as the primary entry, followed by Capsules & Softgels, Tablets, Powders, Liquids, Functional Gum, and Oral Films.
3. **Solutions** — Women’s Wellness; Sleep, Stress & Mood; Beauty From Within; Gut & Digestive Health; Daily Essentials; Active Nutrition.
4. **OEM / ODM** — show formula, sensory, format, packaging, sampling, MOQ, and project collaboration.
5. **Manufacturing** — prove R&D, clean production, capacity, packing, and delivery capability.
6. **Quality** — explain traceability, process control, testing, release documents, and certificate scope.
7. **About** — explain the factory-owned VITHELO export identity and manufacturing history.
8. **Resources** — publish buyer education, not generic consumer-health blogging.
9. **Contact** — email and WhatsApp first; project brief second.

## 5. Homepage narrative

The homepage follows one continuous buyer journey:

`Position → Proof → Gummy specialty → Market opportunity → Format/MOQ fit → Custom development → Manufacturing proof → Quality evidence → Project process → Channel fit → Contact`

### 01. Hero

**Task:** Explain who VITHELO is, what it makes, and how to start.

- Preserve the existing full-bleed background composition.
- Copy sits over natural negative space on the left.
- Product subjects stay on the right and lower-right.
- Do not convert the composition into a structural 50/50 split.
- Eyebrow: `GUMMY-LED NUTRITION OEM / ODM`.
- Recommended headline: `Build Your Next Nutrition Product With a Proven Manufacturer.`
- Supporting copy identifies U.S. nutrition brands, gummy-led manufacturing, supporting formats, and the route from formula to delivery.
- Primary CTA: `Contact Sales` leading to email/WhatsApp choices.
- Secondary CTA: `Explore Gummy Manufacturing`.
- Trust facts may appear at the bottom: Since 2008; 5,000+ clients; 50+ markets; gummy MOQ from 500 bottles.

**Hero media:**

- Desktop: 2560×1120 px or larger, WebP/JPEG, approximately 2.28:1.
- Left 38–42% must stay visually quiet for copy.
- Right side contains 3–5 VITHELO or neutral sample gummy containers with a softly focused R&D or clean-manufacturing environment.
- Mobile: separate 1200×1600 px, 3:4 composition; subjects in the lower half, copy space above.
- A desktop image may not be crudely cropped into the mobile image.

### 02. Proof Strip

**Task:** Provide immediate, restrained business proof.

- `2008` — Manufacturing experience since.
- `5,000+` — Cumulative clients served.
- `50+` — Countries and markets.
- `500` — Bottles, custom gummy MOQ.
- Use typography and hairlines, not icon cards.
- Do not animate numbers.
- Certification logos do not belong in this strip.

### 03. Gummy Spotlight

**Task:** Demonstrate gummy customization depth without repeating market categories.

- Preserve the existing dark, immersive product zone.
- Replace four DTC product cards with one continuous product stage.
- Four selectable chapters:
  1. Shape & Weight
  2. Flavor & Texture
  3. Formula Development
  4. Packaging Systems
- Only one chapter is emphasized at a time.
- Remove `Featured`, `New`, and invented SKU codes.
- Do not autoplay.
- Mobile becomes a static vertical sequence so every fact remains accessible.
- Closing fact: `Custom gummy MOQ from 500 bottles`, with the condition that final MOQ depends on formula and packaging.

**Media:** Four 2000×1200 px real-image sets with subjects in the right 55% and copy space on the left. Required subjects: shape assortment; flavor/color macro; R&D sampling; unbranded or VITHELO packaging.

### 04. Market-ready Solutions

**Task:** Let buyers recognize growth directions before choosing a final formula.

Use six normal-scroll cinematic chapters, one full-width scene per direction:

1. Women’s Wellness
2. Sleep, Stress & Mood
3. Beauty From Within
4. Gut & Digestive Health
5. Daily Essentials
6. Active Nutrition

Each chapter contains a market-direction label, one short headline, a short development description, and a link to the relevant solution page. Copy and subject placement alternate left/right to create rhythm.

- This is a product-development direction, not a finished-product efficacy catalog.
- No medical, disease, cure, weight-loss, hormone-balancing, or guaranteed-result claims.
- No Amazon-rank, hot-sale, or sales-volume badges.
- No scroll lock, forced snap, or altered scroll speed.
- Animation is decorative only; reduced motion shows a static final state.

**Media:** Six 2400×1400 px horizontal images with alternating copy space. Product must be VITHELO or neutral/unbranded. Free commercial photography may support environments or people but must not make another brand’s packaging the subject.

### 05. Dosage Form Spectrum

**Task:** Allow rapid format and MOQ comparison.

Show all formats on one continuous material shelf rather than eight cards:

| Format | Public custom MOQ |
|---|---:|
| Gummies | 500 bottles |
| Hard Capsules | 60,000–100,000 pieces |
| Softgels | 300,000 pieces |
| Tablets | 100,000 pieces |
| Powders | 100 kg |
| Functional Gum | 2 metric tons |
| Liquids | Contact us for MOQ |
| Oral Films | Contact us for MOQ |

- Gummies receive the only accent-color emphasis.
- The page must state that final MOQ depends on formula and packaging.
- Desktop shows a continuous comparison shelf.
- Mobile uses user-controlled horizontal browsing with visible direction cues and no autoplay.

**Media:** Eight transparent PNG/WebP product objects, long edge approximately 1200 px, photographed at a consistent angle, light, and shadow.

### 06. Custom Development

**Task:** Explain what is coordinated within one OEM/ODM project.

Use one product-configuration blueprint around a central VITHELO or neutral sample:

1. Formula — ingredient and dosage feasibility.
2. Sensory — flavor, color, and texture.
3. Format — shape, size, unit weight, and serving direction.
4. Packaging — container, label, and retail presentation.

- Do not promise that every active ingredient works in gummies.
- Do not invent sampling time.
- Do not describe VITHELO as providing U.S. legal approval.
- Do not imply packaging design is free.

**Media:** One transparent central product image at approximately 1600×2000 px. If final packaging is unavailable, use a neutral sample clearly labeled `Sample presentation`.

### 07. Manufacturing Proof

**Task:** Prove repeatable production and scale.

- One full-width real clean-production panorama.
- Headline: `Built to move from formulation to repeatable production.`
- Selected annual capacity ledger:
  - Gummies: 5,000+ metric tons/year.
  - Hard capsules: 54B+ pieces/year.
  - Tablets: 36B+ pieces/year.
- Other factory capacity belongs on the Manufacturing page.
- The `54B+` and `36B+` English conversions must be rechecked against original Chinese factory records before publication.
- Do not use stock-factory photography as if it were VITHELO’s factory.

**Media:** Homepage panorama at 2560×1440 px with copy-safe area on the left. Manufacturing page should later receive laboratory, clean workshop, extraction, liquid/filling, and packaging photography at approximately 1800×1200 px.

### 08. Quality & Compliance

**Task:** Explain quality as a batch evidence trail rather than a logo wall.

Present a designed `Batch Quality Record` structure:

1. Raw Material Traceability
2. In-process Controls
3. Finished-product Testing
4. Release & Export Documents

Documentation that may be listed for review after verification: GMP, HACCP, ISO 22000, ISO 9001, Halal, COA, third-party testing, and applicable U.S. regulatory documentation.

Before publication, verify the certificate holder, production site, scope, issuer, certificate number, validity, and permission to publish.

Never use:

- `FDA Certified`
- `100% Safe`
- `Guaranteed Compliance`
- A claim that one certificate applies to all sites, markets, or products
- Fake values, certificate numbers, signatures, or seals

### 09. Six-step Project Runway

**Task:** Make the buying process actionable.

1. Project Brief
2. Formula Development
3. Sample Approval
4. Packaging Setup
5. Production
6. Inspection & Delivery

Use one continuous project runway rather than six cards. Selecting a milestone shows:

- what the buyer provides;
- what VITHELO returns;
- what decision closes the stage.

Do not autoplay or promise a fixed lead time. Mobile becomes a vertical project runway with each stage readable in order.

### 10. Built for Your Channel

**Task:** Translate the same manufacturing base into three buyer-specific routes.

Use one branching channel-network diagram:

- **Nutrition Brands:** formula differentiation, sensory signature, branded packaging system, portfolio growth.
- **E-commerce Sellers:** flexible gummy entry, SKU/packaging coordination, label-file workflow, reorder planning.
- **Retail & Supermarkets:** retail packaging requirements, multi-SKU coordination, volume planning, quality-document support.

Do not use Amazon, TikTok Shop, Shopify, or supermarket logos. Do not imply official partnership, marketplace approval, account operation, or unverified retail placement.

### 11. Final Contact + Footer

**Task:** Complete the sales conversion and provide reliable site navigation.

Final contact hierarchy:

1. Email Our Sales Team — direct.
2. Talk on WhatsApp — direct.
3. Submit a Project Brief — structured secondary route.

Approved contact data remains `NOT_CONFIGURED` until the user supplies it. Do not invent email, phone, response time, address, or working hours.

The project brief is a dedicated form or lightweight modal, not a large footer form. Fields:

- Required: Name, Company, Work Email, Country, Product Direction, Dosage Form.
- Optional: Estimated Quantity, Packaging Requirement, Formula Requirement, Target Timeline, Project Notes.

Footer groups:

- Products
- Solutions
- Capabilities
- Company
- Legal

Do not display unconfigured social accounts. Preserve the existing large VITHELO reveal and background rise as decorative closure only. All contact actions and critical facts must appear before the animation. Reduced Motion shows the final state immediately. No scroll lock or forced dwell.

## 6. Visual system

- Premium scientific manufacturing, not traditional green-health styling.
- Deep Navy `#102A43`, Mineral Gray `#52606D`, Precision Orange `#E6772E`, white, and restrained warm neutral surfaces.
- Orange is reserved for inquiry actions and limited emphasis.
- Do not copy Seed’s green palette, packaging, copy, product logic, or code.
- Avoid generic rounded-card grids, excessive shadows, icon walls, visual clutter, floating decorative pills, and stock-health collages.
- Prefer full-bleed photography, typographic evidence, continuous stages, diagrams, and material-led product imagery.
- Maintain an 8 px spacing system and responsive composition.

## 7. Motion and accessibility

- Motion qualities: precise, smooth, professional, minimal.
- Default interaction transition: approximately 300 ms ease-out.
- Entry motion may use opacity and small vertical translation.
- No bounce, elastic, spring, autoplay carousel, autoplay video, scroll-jacking, or essential information hidden behind animation.
- All selectors and chapter controls require keyboard operation, visible focus, semantic labels, logical order, and minimum 44 px targets.
- Reduced Motion must remove large translations, scaling, and staged reveals while leaving all content present.
- Text resizing, high contrast, non-color status, and mobile reading order must be verified.

## 8. Data and compliance boundaries

Confirmed facts originate from factory materials supplied and confirmed by the user, but publication still requires an evidence file for every date, client count, market count, capacity, certificate, and regulatory statement.

The following remain prohibited unless separately supplied and verified:

- Product efficacy and disease claims.
- Exact formula and dosage claims.
- Certification scope beyond the named site and product coverage.
- Fixed lead time.
- Product price.
- Warranty, shipping, returns, or market policy.
- Customer logos, testimonials, sales results, or marketplace relationships.

Missing facts remain visibly marked `NOT_CONFIGURED` in design and content records.

## 9. Image sourcing policy

- Do not generate images with AI.
- Prioritize real VITHELO/factory product, laboratory, production, packaging, and document photography.
- Free commercial stock may support temporary lifestyle or neutral laboratory backgrounds only.
- Stock images must never impersonate the VITHELO factory, customer, product, or certification.
- Reference images are design references, not source assets.
- Remove or crop third-party brand marks only when the underlying image is a VITHELO-owned factory asset and the user has confirmed authorization; otherwise do not use the image publicly.

## 10. Technical and content constraints

- First implementation remains an HTML5, Tailwind CSS v3, and vanilla JavaScript site unless the user changes the technical direction.
- Preserve the existing homepage file and evolve it surgically.
- Do not introduce React, Vue, or Next.js for this project.
- Use semantic HTML, page metadata, Open Graph data, meaningful alt text, and optimized responsive images.
- Critical product, MOQ, quality, contact, and recovery information must remain accessible without JavaScript.
- No external service integration is authorized yet for forms, CRM, CMS, analytics, consent, or deployment.

## 11. Verification criteria

The homepage design is implemented successfully only when:

- A U.S. buyer can identify the company, core format, OEM/ODM role, and contact route within the Hero.
- Gummies are the visual specialty without hiding other formats.
- All eight dosage formats and approved MOQ values match this specification.
- Manufacturing and quality claims link to verified supporting data.
- Email, WhatsApp, and project brief have distinct, clear roles.
- The page contains no consumer-commerce behavior, copied Seed assets, invented claim, or unverified certification language.
- Desktop and mobile preserve the same buyer decision logic.
- Keyboard, focus, target size, text resize, and Reduced Motion requirements pass.
- There is no overlap, clipping, horizontal overflow, scroll lock, or inaccessible essential content.

## 12. Deferred inputs

The following must be supplied before final implementation or publication:

- Approved VITHELO logo/wordmark usage.
- Sales email.
- WhatsApp number and preferred deep-link format.
- Factory address and public business details.
- Certificate scans and certificate-scope verification.
- Source records for all capacity and company-history figures.
- Real Hero, gummy, factory, laboratory, packaging, and product-object photography.
- Approved privacy, terms, accessibility, and cookie documents.
- Form handling, CRM, analytics, consent, and hosting decisions.
