# Missing Production Inputs

The implemented site is a complete demonstration, not a production launch. The following inputs are required before the corresponding placeholders, adapters, or disabled tasks can become live.

## VITHELO inquiry launch blockers

- VITHELO inquiry email address, owner, and expected response policy.
- VITHELO WhatsApp number in E.164 format, account owner, supported hours, and privacy handling.
- Approved desktop and mobile nutrition Hero imagery, including art direction, crop, focal point, alt text, and usage rights.
- Approved product sheets, capability statements, market eligibility, and inquiry-routing rules.

## Brand identity

- Approved production vector masters for the supplied VITHELO wordmark and V monogram.
- Final wordmark, logo, symbol, lockups, clear-space rules, and usage rights.
- Licensed production fonts and approved typography roles.
- Approved Optical accent and full brand color accessibility review.
- Approved voice, naming, legal naming, and locale-specific brand copy.

## Real Nutrition SKU data

- Product IDs, slugs, names, descriptors, variants, validated health categories, forms, pack sizes, and availability.
- Formula and ingredient records with approved relationships.
- Approved usage, amount, timing, frequency, storage, suitability, warnings, and contraindications.
- Price, currency, inventory, subscription decision if any, tax, and market availability.
- Approved packaging, labels, batch, product-support source material, and product media for each nutrition SKU.
- Approved capsule form media and transparent ruby-red bear-gummy form media, with rights, alt text, dimensions, and replacement policy.

## Real device data

- Device IDs, slugs, names, models, variants, included items, and complete specifications.
- Approved intended use, technology, mechanism, modes, settings, outputs, controls, and human-interface records.
- Approved manual covering setup, operation, duration, supervision, cleaning, storage, maintenance, and aftercare.
- Safety warnings, contraindications, escalation, service, registration, warranty, and ownership support.
- Regulatory and market-eligibility records supplied with source and scope.

## Approved science and claims

- Approved evidence sources with citations or source documents.
- Scope, population, method, limitations, and product relationships for every evidence record.
- Approved supported-statement boundaries and exact market-specific claim language.
- Certification, testing, quality-system, facility, manufacturing, and traceability evidence where applicable.
- Named content owner and approval workflow for Science, Claims, Quality, and Safety.

## Market and locale

- Launch markets, languages, locale rules, currencies, units, and tax context.
- Market-specific catalog, price, availability, claims, Safety, eligibility, and support differences.
- Required legal, consent, accessibility, and regulated-product review for each market.
- Translation ownership and review process.

## Commerce, CMS, and CRM

- Commerce provider and contracts for catalog, price, inventory, cart, checkout, payment, tax, shipping, orders, and returns.
- CMS choice and content lifecycle for product, evidence, Safety, media, and editorial records.
- CRM or inquiry provider, qualification fields, consent, routing, retention, and response ownership.
- Search provider, indexing scope, ranking rules, and empty/error behavior.
- Identity provider and requirements for account, saved items, addresses, orders, and owned products.
- Analytics and consent requirements if tracking is authorized.

## Media

- Approved product packshots and complete device views.
- Approved adult human-rhythms media/poster, routine, skin-interface, professional, factory, and manufacturing media.
- Asset rights, territories, expiry, alt text, crops, focal points, dimensions, and delivery formats.
- Approved diagrams, manuals, evidence figures, and Safety illustrations.
- Policy for replacing the current demonstration images in `public/media/`.

## Policies and operations

- Shipping regions, methods, rates, delivery estimates, and restrictions.
- Returns, refunds, exchanges, cancellation, and damaged-item policies.
- Nutrition and device warranty terms.
- Device service, cleaning, maintenance, repair, registration, and escalation paths.
- Privacy, terms, cookies, consent, payment, tax, and regulated-product notices.
- Support providers, hours, service levels, contact channels, and ownership.
- Professional MOQ, lead time, capacity, project stages, commercial terms, and inquiry response process if approved.

## Transition rule

Do not fill these gaps with assumptions. Keep `DEMO_ONLY` and `NOT_CONFIGURED` until each value has a source, owner, market context, approval state, schema mapping, adapter pathway, and acceptance evidence.
