# Product

<!-- impeccable:product-schema 1 -->

## Platform

web

## Stack

Delegated and confirmed through the approved design spec: Next.js App Router, TypeScript, Tailwind CSS 4, selectively customized shadcn/Radix primitives, Motion, Zod, Vitest, Testing Library, and Playwright.

## Users

- Design-conscious consumers evaluating nutrition products or aesthetic devices and needing clear product, usage, safety, and trust information.
- Professional buyers evaluating OEM/ODM, private label, distribution, or professional aesthetic capability and needing a clear project path.

## Product Purpose

Create one premium digital brand platform that connects internal nutrition, external aesthetic technology, scientific trust, commerce, and professional business capability. Success means visitors can orient, discover, evaluate, trust, convert, and continue without confusing the two product worlds or weakening factual boundaries.

## Positioning

One master brand organizes two product worlds through one human system and one trust system. It is not a stitched nutrition shop plus device catalog.

## Operating Context

The site supports brand discovery, product comparison, product decision, evidence reading, safety discovery, cart/checkout demo flows, post-purchase support, and professional project intake. It will initially run with local demo fixtures and provider-neutral integration boundaries.

## Capabilities and Constraints

- Primary navigation: Nutrition, Aesthetic Technology, By Need, Science, Professional.
- Core prototype routes: Home, two landings, two PDPs, Science, Professional, Search, Cart, Checkout, Account, Support.
- Product, Formula, Technology, Evidence, Safety, Professional, Market, and Commerce data remain separated and schema-driven.
- All unavailable facts use explicit `DEMO_ONLY` or `NOT_CONFIGURED` states.
- No real payment, inquiry sending, deployment, claims, certification, product parameters, or market policies are authorized.
- Mobile is a priority model, not a scaled desktop composition.

## Brand Commitments

- A-Prime / Scientific Material Humanism.
- HUMAN × MATERIAL × PRECISION.
- One brand and two product worlds, not two visual brands.
- Calm, precise, human, progressive, elevated voice.
- Seed is a structural benchmark only. Do not copy its green microbiome identity, wording, product logic, layout, motion, or code.
- Wordmark-first. The temporary wordmark is replaceable and must not be mistaken for a final brand name.

## Product Principles

1. Say less and prove more.
2. Commerce first on PDPs, with brand in support.
3. Explain first and archive later in Science.
4. Component-first implementation and one source of truth.
5. Critical safety, accessibility, and state completeness are completion criteria.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

Keyboard operation, visible focus, logical focus order, semantic labels, 44px touch targets, contrast, screen-reader structure, text resizing, non-color-only states, and reduced motion are required. Critical safety and core transaction information may not be hidden for visual neatness.
